Conditionals & Probability
Tryb Warunkowy i Wyrażanie Prawdopodobieństwa
The two advanced conditional types — hypothetical and counterfactual — plus the vocabulary of probability and uncertainty. These are the tools of economic forecasting, policy analysis, and nuanced argumentation at the highest register.
Type 2 Conditional — Hypothetical Present/Future
The Type 2 conditional says: "if X were the case (it isn't), then Y would happen." The condition is hypothetical or unlikely — it refers to the present or future. In Polish: gdyby + past form → conditional (-łby/-łaby).
If-clause (gdyby)

gdyby + past tense form (looks like past, acts like conditional subordinate clause)

Gdyby rząd zainwestował więcej…

If the government were to invest more…

Gdyby tempo wzrostu utrzymało się…

If the growth rate were maintained…

Main clause (-łby / -łaby)

conditional form: past stem + -by + person/gender ending

…dysproporcje zmalałyby szybciej.

…disparities would decrease faster.

…Polska dogoniłaby Niemcy.

…Poland would catch up with Germany.

Full examples — Type 2

Gdyby inflacja spadła, stopy procentowe mogłyby zostać obniżone.

If inflation were to fall, interest rates could be lowered.

Gdybyśmy zainwestowali w reedukację, rynek pracy byłby bardziej elastyczny.

If we were to invest in retraining, the labour market would be more flexible.

Key signal: the situation described does NOT currently exist — it's a hypothesis about what would happen IF things were different now or in the future.

Type 3 Conditional — Counterfactual Past
The Type 3 conditional says: "if X had happened (it didn't), then Y would have happened." The condition refers to the past — it is definitively counterfactual. In Polish: gdyby + past form → byłby/byłoby + past participle OR past conditional.
If-clause (gdyby + past)

Same form as Type 2! Context and logic tell you it's past.

Gdyby Polska nie przystąpiła do UE…

If Poland had not joined the EU… (but she DID)

Gdyby środki nie zostały zainwestowane…

If the funds had not been invested… (but they were)

Main clause (byłoby / conditional)

byłby/byłaby/byłoby + adjective/participle OR past conditional

…PKB byłoby niższe.

…GDP would have been lower.

…infrastruktura pozostałaby na poziomie komunizmu.

…infrastructure would have remained at communist levels.

Full examples — Type 3

Gdybyśmy nie weszli do UE, nie otrzymalibyśmy funduszy strukturalnych.

If we had not joined the EU, we would not have received structural funds.

Gdyby reforma nie została przeprowadzona, gospodarka byłaby znacznie słabsza.

If the reform had not been carried out, the economy would have been considerably weaker.

Key signal: we know the real past — the conditional imagines an alternative history that did NOT happen.

Probability Expressions — Wyrażanie Prawdopodobieństwa
Economic and political analysis requires precise gradation of certainty. Polish has a rich spectrum of probability expressions — choosing the right one signals both linguistic competence and intellectual precision.
na pewno / z pewnością
certainly / definitely (100%)

najprawdopodobniej

prawdopodobnie
