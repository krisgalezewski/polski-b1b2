Grammar Unit G1
The Case System: A Complete Map
Przypadki — Przegląd wszystkich 7 przypadków
Polish has 7 grammatical cases. You have already encountered several in the reading — this unit gives you the full map, explains when each case is used, and shows how they appear in work and career vocabulary. End with the diagnostic quiz to identify your weakest cases.
The 7 Cases at a Glance
Every Polish noun, pronoun and adjective changes its ending depending on its grammatical role in the sentence. The ending change is called declension, and the role is called a case. Each case answers a specific question and is triggered by specific verbs and prepositions.

Instrumental Case — most important for work vocabulary
The Instrumental appears constantly in professional Polish. It is triggered by key verbs and the preposition z (together with).
1. After być / zostać — role and identity (Instrumental)

Jestem kierownikiem. — I am a manager. ✓ (not "kierownik")

Została dyrektorką. — She became a director.

After być / zostać, the role takes Instrumental — never Nominative.

⚠ Exception: pracować jako — Nominative!

Pracuję jako kierownik. — I work as a manager. ✓ (Nominative)

"Pracować jako" is an exception — the role stays in the Nominative, unlike after "być/zostać". Think of "jako" as introducing a comparison or label, not an identity transformation.

2. After zarządzać, kierować, zajmować się, interesować się

Zarządza zespołem. (zespół → zespołem)

Kieruje projektem. — He leads the project.

Zajmuje się marketingiem. — She deals with marketing.

Locative Case — location and topic
Core prepositions → always Locative

w: w firmie, w Warszawie, w dziale — in the company / Warsaw / department

na: na spotkaniu, na szkoleniu — at a meeting / training

o: mówić o projekcie, myśleć o karierze — talk about the project / think about career

przy: przy biurku, przy komputerze — at the desk / computer
