Unit 8 › Grammar Focus
Grammar Unit G7 · Part 2
Future Tense, Conditional & Directional Cases
Czas Przyszły, Tryb Warunkowy i Przypadki Kierunkowe
Three grammar systems that are essential for talking about travel: expressing what will happen (two ways), making polite requests, and describing movement and direction with the correct cases.
Future Tense — Czas Przyszły (Two Forms)
Polish has two future tense forms, directly linked to verbal aspect. Choosing the right one depends on whether you want to express a completed event or an ongoing process.
1. Perfective future — single completed event

Pociąg wyjedzie o 7:15. — The train will depart at 7:15.

Anna dotrze do Krakowa wieczorem. — Anna will reach Kraków in the evening.

Czy kupisz bilet online? — Will you buy the ticket online?

Form: use the present tense conjugation of the perfective verb — it automatically means future. No "będę" needed.

2. Imperfective future — ongoing process or repeated action

Przez trzy godziny będę jechać autobusem. — For three hours I will be travelling by bus.

Ona będzie zwiedzać miasto cały dzień. — She will be sightseeing the city all day.

Będziesz czekać na mnie, prawda? — You will be waiting for me, right?

Form: będę/będziesz/będzie/będziemy/będziecie/będą + infinitive (impf.) OR + past form of impf. verb.

Conditional for Polite Requests — Tryb Warunkowy
The conditional mood (formed with past tense + -by) is used in Polish for polite requests, wishes, and hypothetical situations. It is far more common in spoken Polish than English speakers expect.
1. Polite wishes — chciałbym / chciałabym (I would like)

Chciałbym kupić bilet do Warszawy. — I would like to buy a ticket to Warsaw. (masc.)

Chciałabym zarezerwować miejscówkę. — I would like to reserve a seat. (fem.)

More polite than "chcę" (I want). Gender-specific: -łbym (masc.), -łabym (fem.).

2. Polite requests — Czy mógłby pan / mogłaby pani…?

Czy mógłby pan powiedzieć, z którego peronu odjeżdża pociąg?

Could you (masc. formal) tell me which platform the train leaves from?

Czy mogłaby pani sprawdzić mój bilet?

Could you (fem. formal) check my ticket?

Structure: Czy + mógłby/mogłaby/mogliby + pan/pani/państwo + infinitive?

3. Hypothetical conditions — gdybym miał/miała…

Gdybym miała więcej czasu, zwiedziłabym całe miasto.

If I had more time, I would visit the whole city.

Gdybyś kupił bilet wcześniej, byłoby taniej.

If you had bought the ticket earlier, it would have been cheaper.

Form: gdyby + past tense (conditional clause) + -by form (main clause). Relates to Unit 7's purpose clauses — both use past-tense forms in subordinate clauses.

Key contrast: dokąd vs gdzie vs skąd

Gdzie jesteś? — Where are you? (static location)

Dokąd jedziesz? — Where are you going (to)? (direction)

Skąd jedziesz? — Where are you coming from? (origin)

This three-way distinction has no direct parallel in English. "Where" in English covers all three; Polish requires different words.
