Locative Case & Prepositions of Place
Miejscownik i Przyimki Miejsca
The Locative is the only Polish case that never appears without a preposition. Master the key prepositions, their cases, and the crucial location-vs-direction distinction.
The Locative Case — Miejscownik
The Locative answers gdzie? o czym? o kim? (where? about what? about whom?). It always follows a preposition — it never stands alone. Key prepositions: w, na, przy, o, po, po.

The Key Distinction: Location vs Direction
This is one of the most important — and most commonly confused — patterns in Polish spatial language.
MeaningPreposition + CaseExampleEnglish
Location: insidew + LocativeJestem w domu.I am at home (inside).
Direction: todo + GenitiveIdę do domu.I'm going home (towards).
Location: on surfacena + LocativeLeży na stole.It's lying on the table.
Direction: ontona + AccusativeKładę na stół.I'm putting (it) onto the table.
Location: next toprzy + LocativeStoję przy oknie.I'm standing by the window.
Origin: from insidez + GenitiveWychodzę z domu.I'm leaving the house.

Memory tip: Location = Locative (static, where something IS). Direction = different case (dynamic, where something GOES or COMES FROM).
