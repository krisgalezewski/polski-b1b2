About this Course
📖
Course Structure
  • 12 thematic topic units covering everyday Polish life
  • 8 integrated grammar units (G1–G8)
  • 4 combined unit tests + 2 final assessments
  • Progressive difficulty: full scaffolding in Units 1–4, reduced in 5–8, none in 9–12
🎯
Each Unit Contains
  • Extended lesson: reading → comprehension → listening → writing
  • Integrated grammar reference & exercises
  • Gap fill (with word bank & distractors)
  • Multiple choice (8 questions)
  • Matching (vocabulary pairs)
  • Unit test (unlocked on completion)
🤖
AI Writing Assessment
Every lesson ends with an AI-assessed writing task. Claude analyses your text for vocabulary use, case accuracy, tense, aspect, and grammar — giving personalised feedback with specific error corrections and a score out of 100.
🔊
Pronunciation
Every vocabulary item has a native-language audio button using your device's built-in Polish text-to-speech. Works offline on iPhone and MacBook with no extra cost.
📈
Progressive Difficulty
  • Units 1–4 Full scaffolding — audio, word banks, English hints, visible transcripts
  • Units 5–8 Reduced — no word bank, grammar-label hints, transcript hidden
  • Units 9–12 No scaffolding — authentic texts, longest writing tasks
📱
Works Everywhere
Each unit is a standalone HTML file that opens in any browser — Safari on iPhone, Safari or Chrome on MacBook. No app store, no login, no subscription. The AI writing feature requires an internet connection; everything else works offline.
